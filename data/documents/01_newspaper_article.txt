Title: Let Mind Think And You Simply Be
Author: Swami Bhoomananda Tirtha
Category: Spirituality and Self-Realisation

ARTICLE CONTENT :

There was a time when I thought that meditation was everything in spiritual life. Simultaneously, I also did self-introspection. But I found that both these were inadequate. Why was I meditating? What was I trying to get? Though the Self, or Self-realisation, is the pursuit, the Self is not something to be obtained. The description of Self is: Oneself is the Self. If I am the Self, where is the question of getting it? And as the Self, I cannot be different. I was not different, I have not become different, I will not be different. The Self, non-different from me, was only to be understood and realised as such.

In ordinary interactional life, all experiences occur in one area, and that area is within the body. I know that I am. I woke up, I dreamt, I slept. I see, I hear. All these experiences are taking place somewhere within me. It is in that very place that Self-experience, the knowledge that “I am the Self”, should also be had.

Is the Self to be experienced? No, it is already there. We are already experiencing it. Then why do I feel that I am different from the Self? Is the Self to be realised, or is it the feeling that “I am different” to be overcome and dissolved? Desires and various allied notions are redundant; the mind holds the key to everything. The mind should be made clear, light and cheerful, and all expectations and desires are to be dropped.

We should not be troubled by mind. Why does mind get ruffled? That’s because something is wrong with its function. We do not accept reality. Or we doubt, expect, hope with a clinging mind. Drop all these things. The mind is your friend. Let the mind think. Accept whatever thoughts are useful and necessary. You will postpone some thoughts and act on them later. When thoughts are bad or undesirable, simply acknowledge them. That is sufficient.

When you understand something to be bad, simply reject it. Dissuade bad thoughts because you are not interested in them. After a few occurrences, they will stop coming.

What is sadhana? It is to be at home with your mind and see that it is pure, calm, gentle, delightful, and poised. By being straightforward, you can speed up this process. You have to be honest, liberal. Understand that for Self-experience or Self-realisation to take place, the mind should be pure, free of stinginess, small-mindedness, and desires.

In this way, you have to find your own method. Then keep improving until you have a beautiful mind. In front of God, cry or weep whenever you want. But every time you weep, you should find purity and relief. When you meditate or sit in solitude, all you have to do is be with the mind. Let the mind think, and you simply be. Do not grumble. Do not be egoistic. Do not be selfish. Allow all thoughts to subside. This is sadhana.
